After the Unthinkable: Your First Steps Following a Car Accident in Bucks County (Before Calling a Collision Shop)

Published July 31, 2025 · Updated October 9, 2026

One second you’re driving along Route 132 or cutting through County Line Road, and the next, everything stops. A car accident, no matter how small, can shake you. In the moments right after, it’s hard to think clearly. What you do in those first few minutes matters, and having a plan helps you regain control.

Safety Comes Before Everything

Take a deep breath. Check yourself and your passengers for injuries. If anyone is hurt, call 911 right away. When the vehicles are drivable, pull off to the shoulder or into a safe parking area. Turn on your flashers to alert other drivers.

Even in minor fender benders, adrenaline runs high. Pause for a second to steady yourself. Calm decisions now can make the rest of the process much easier.

Gather the Right Information

Before tow trucks or traffic clear the scene, document everything you can. Exchange names, contact info, and insurance details with the other driver. Use your phone to snap:

  • Close-ups and wide shots of the damage
  • The surrounding area, including traffic signs or skid marks
  • License plates of all vehicles involved

These photos become valuable later. Not just for insurance, but also for the collision shop that will assess the damage.

Call Your Insurance Company Early

Once everyone is safe and the scene is under control, contact your insurance provider. Stick to the facts when you report the accident and share the photos you’ve taken. There’s no need to commit to any repairs just yet. Your goal right now is simply to start the claim and create a clear record of the incident.

Hold Off on Choosing a Repair Shop Immediately

After a crash, tow truck operators and roadside assistance might suggest a shop on the spot. Take your time. You have the right to choose where your car gets repaired. Waiting until you can speak with a trusted local collision center ensures you’re making a decision that’s right for you, not just convenient in the moment.

When You’re Ready for Repairs

Once the initial chaos settles, that’s the time to call a collision repair specialist.

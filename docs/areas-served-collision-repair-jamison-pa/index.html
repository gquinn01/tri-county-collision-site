For drivers from Jamison

This page is for anyone who lives or works in Jamison and needs a car fixed after an accident. We are not in Jamison, and we won't pretend to be: Tri-County Collision is a family-owned shop in Southampton, and we repair cars for drivers from Jamison and from its neighbors Warminster, Richboro and Ivyland.

Jamison is in Warwick Township, Bucks County, where York Road (PA 263) crosses Almshouse Road. From there the shop is about 8 miles away by road, and about 15 minutes without traffic.

If your car has been in an accident, call before you decide where it goes. Estimates are free and there is no obligation.

Getting to the shop from Jamison

From the crossroads of York Road and Almshouse Road, the drive is about 8.4 miles and takes about 15 minutes without traffic.

  1. Head south on York Road (PA 263) for about 2 miles.
  2. Turn left onto West Bristol Road and follow it for about 4 miles.
  3. Turn right onto Second Street Pike (PA 232) and go south for about 2 miles.
  4. Turn right onto Jaymor Rd, and the shop is about a quarter mile along, at 995 Jaymor Rd, Southampton, PA 18966.

Staying on York Road to County Line Road, then following County Line Road east to James Way and Jaymor Rd, takes about the same time. There is a map on our contact page.

Questions from Jamison drivers

Is Tri-County Collision actually in Jamison?

Tri-County Collision is not in Jamison: the shop is at 995 Jaymor Rd, Southampton, PA 18966, about 8 miles and 15 minutes from Jamison without traffic. Pennsylvania law protects your right to choose your own collision shop for repairs. Your insurance company cannot require you to use their preferred facility or steer you elsewhere. Our post on your right to choose a body shop explains the law.

How do I get to Tri-County Collision from Jamison?

The drive from Jamison to Tri-County Collision is about 8.4 miles and takes about 15 minutes without traffic: south on York Road (PA 263), left on West Bristol Road, right on Second Street Pike (PA 232), and right on Jaymor Rd to the shop.

Does Tri-County Collision work with my insurance company if I live in Jamison?

Tri-County Collision works with all major insurance companies, and a Jamison address makes no difference to that.

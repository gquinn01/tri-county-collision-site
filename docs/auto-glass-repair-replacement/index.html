Repair or Replace? Here's How We Decide

Not every damaged windshield needs to be replaced, and we won't sell you a replacement you don't need. When you bring your vehicle in, our technicians assess the damage and give you a precise estimate for the work your glass actually requires.

Windshield Repair

Small chips and short cracks can often be repaired, saving you the cost of a full replacement. Repairing damage early also stops it from spreading. Temperature swings, potholes, and everyday driving can all turn a repairable chip into a crack that isn't.

Windshield Replacement

Some damage is past the point of repair: long cracks, damage in your line of sight, or cracks that keep growing. When replacement is the right call, we install quality glass with the same care we bring to every repair. A properly installed windshield matters to your safety, and we treat it that way.

Side and Rear Windows

A shattered door window or rear window can't wait. It leaves your vehicle open to the weather and to anyone walking by. Our technicians are experienced with all types of auto glass and can replace side and rear windows to get your vehicle sealed up and secure again.

Newer Vehicle? Ask About ADAS Recalibration

Many newer vehicles mount cameras and sensors behind the windshield to power safety features like lane departure warning and automatic emergency braking. After a windshield replacement, those systems often need to be recalibrated. We perform ADAS recalibration at our Southampton facility, so your vehicle's safety features work the way the manufacturer intended after the new glass goes in.

Auto Glass Repair & Replacement FAQ

Can my chipped windshield be repaired, or does it need replacement?

Whether a chipped windshield can be repaired or needs replacement depends on the size, depth, and location of the damage. Small chips caught early can often be repaired; longer cracks, spreading damage, and anything in your direct line of sight usually call for replacement. Bring it in and we'll assess it. You'll get a straight answer and a precise estimate.

Is it safe to drive with a cracked windshield?

Driving with a cracked windshield is a risk that grows with time. Cracks can spread quickly and interfere with your visibility, and your windshield plays a real role in protecting you. Our advice: have the damage looked at promptly, while a smaller (and less expensive) repair may still be an option.

Do you replace side and rear windows too?

Yes, our technicians handle all types of auto glass: windshields, side windows, and rear windows.

Will insurance cover my auto glass repair or replacement?

Whether insurance covers your auto glass repair or replacement depends on your coverage. We work with your insurance company directly and help make the claims process as painless as possible, so you're not stuck playing middleman.

Does my windshield need recalibration after it's replaced?

Your windshield very likely needs recalibration after it's replaced if your vehicle has camera-based safety features like lane departure warning, automatic emergency braking, or adaptive cruise control. We perform ADAS recalibration so those systems work the way the manufacturer intended after your new windshield is installed.
